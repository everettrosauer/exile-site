Season Zero rules

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version

  • Season Zero is a free, four week pilot. There is no entry fee and no cash prize.
  • You link your real accounts before day one. Every linked account counts for the whole season.
  • Pro is ranked on return against your worst drop. Fall 15% from your peak and your season ends.
  • Everything is shown in percent. Nobody sees your balance.
  • Do not take more risk because of a leaderboard. Most traders lose money.

Who can play

Season Zero is open by invitation to adults 18 and over who live in the United States. One person, one entry.

People who run Exile may link accounts to test the system. They are marked on the board and cannot be sent to a firm.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no entry fee, no deposit and nothing to buy. Exile never holds your money and never places a trade.

Dates

Season Zero runs from Monday, January 4, 2027 to Sunday, January 31, 2027. Accounts must be linked by 11:59 pm Eastern on Sunday, January 3.

Which accounts count

  • Stock and options accounts, crypto accounts and wallets, and prediction market accounts that are in your own name.
  • The list of supported venues is posted here before day one.
  • Prediction market trades on sports do not count toward any board.
  • Every account you link counts from the first day to the last. You cannot add or remove an account during the season.
  • If a connection breaks, you have 72 hours to reconnect it. After that the record is marked incomplete.
  • A record starts on the day you link. Older trades are never added.

How Pro is scored

  • Return is the percent change across all your linked accounts together. Deposits and withdrawals are taken out, so adding money is not profit.
  • Worst drop is the largest fall from a high point during the season.
  • Score is return divided by worst drop. If your worst drop is under 1%, we use 1%.
  • The line sits 15% below your highest point so far. Cross it and you are exiled for the rest of the season. Your record stays on the board, marked exiled.
  • You need at least 10 closed trades to hold a rank.
  • If two scores tie, the smaller worst drop ranks higher.

How Social works

  • You choose which trades to post. Every post is checked against your account.
  • Your profile says Highlights or Full record. A Highlights profile never shows a return, a win rate or a rank.
  • Weekly boards rank single trades: best trade, worst trade and boldest call.

Fair play

  • Trade your own money in your own accounts.
  • Do not hide opposite bets in an account you did not link.
  • Do not trade between your own accounts to make a result look better.
  • Do not enter more than once.

We can remove a record that breaks these rules, and we will say why.

Checks for top finishers

Before a top Pro report is shared with any firm, the trader passes an identity check and hands over season statements for every account they hold at the linked venues. A trader can say no. The record then stays on the board, marked unchecked, and no report is shared.

Each trader decides which firms see their report. Exile does not send a report anywhere without that trader's written yes.

What you can win

Season Zero has no cash prize. Top Pro finishers receive a scouting report on their season. If a trading firm has agreed to look at reports, you choose whether yours goes to it. No firm has agreed yet. Nobody is promised an interview, a job or money to trade.

Risk

Exile keeps score. It does not give advice, and a high rank is not a reason to copy anyone. Trade the way you would without a board. Do not add size, add risk or trade more often to climb it.

Changes

These are pilot rules. We may change them before day one, and the date at the top of this page will change when we do. After day one we change a rule only to fix a mistake or to stop cheating.
