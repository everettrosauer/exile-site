Rules

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version

  • Exile is free for traders. There is no entry fee and no cash prize.
  • You link your real accounts, read only. Your record starts that day and keeps going.
  • Every month, linked traders are ranked on one board. Fall 15% from your peak in a month and you are off that month's board.
  • Everything is shown in percent. Nobody sees your balance.
  • Do not take more risk because of a leaderboard. Most traders lose money.

Where things stand

Account linking is not open yet. These are the rules it will open with.

Who can join

Adults 18 and over who live in the United States. One person, one record.

People who run Exile may link accounts to test the system. Their records are marked, and no report on them goes to a firm.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no fee, no deposit and nothing to buy. Exile never holds your money and never places a trade.

Your record

  • A record starts on the day you link. Older trades are never added.
  • It counts stock and options accounts, crypto accounts and wallets, and prediction market accounts that are in your own name.
  • The list of supported venues will be posted here when linking opens.
  • Prediction market trades on sports do not count.
  • Every account you link counts, every day. Nobody gets to show only the one that won.
  • You can unlink an account at any time. The record stops that day and says so.
  • If a connection breaks, you have 72 hours to reconnect it. After that the record is marked incomplete for those days.

The monthly board

  • The board runs by calendar month, on Eastern time. It resets on the first.
  • To be ranked in a month, you must be linked before that month starts. Link mid month and your record starts right away. Your first ranked month is the next one.
  • Return is the percent change across all your linked accounts together, for the month. Deposits and withdrawals are taken out, so adding money is not profit.
  • Worst drop is the largest fall from a high point during the month.
  • Score is return divided by worst drop. If your worst drop is under 1%, we use 1%.
  • The line sits 15% below your highest point of the month so far. Cross it and you are exiled from that month's board. Your record keeps going, and you are back on the board next month.
  • You need at least 10 closed trades in the month to hold a rank.
  • If two scores tie, the smaller worst drop ranks higher.

Fair play

  • Trade your own money in your own accounts.
  • Do not hide opposite bets in an account you did not link.
  • Do not trade between your own accounts to make a result look better.
  • Do not keep more than one record.

We can remove a record that breaks these rules, and we will say why.

Reports for firms

A scouting report is a one page summary of a record. It covers at least one full month on the board.

Before a report goes to any firm, the trader passes an identity check and hands over statements for every account they hold at the linked venues. A trader can say no. Then no report is shared.

Each trader decides which firms see their report. Exile does not send a report anywhere without that trader's written yes.

No firm has agreed to read reports yet. Nobody is promised an interview, a job or money to trade.

Risk

Exile keeps score. It does not give advice, and a high rank is not a reason to copy anyone. Trade the way you would without a board. Do not add size, add risk or trade more often to climb it.

Changes

Exile is early. We may change these rules, and the date at the top of this page will change when we do. Once linking is open, we will not change how a month is scored after that month has started.
