Size is the money in the trade as a share of the account. Result is what the trade did to that money. Hit is what it did to the whole account. The full ledger of trades comes with the report.

Questions we would ask

    What gets checked

    • One brokerage account, linked before day one. Read only.
    • Every trade is read from the broker. Nothing is typed in.
    • Deposits and withdrawals are taken out of the return.
    • Identity check: a government ID matched to the name on the account.
    • Statement check: the broker's own statements for the season, matched against the linked record.
    • Account size stays private. This trader agreed to share a band: $25,000 to $50,000.

    This is a sample, so nothing here was really checked. In a real report every line is done before the trader shares it.

    What this report can't tell you

    • Whether it lasts. Four weeks shows how someone handles risk. It does not prove an edge.
    • What happened in accounts the trader never linked.
    • Why each trade was made. That is what the interview is for.
    • How this style holds up at ten times the size.
