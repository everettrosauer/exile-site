Questions we would ask

    What was checked

    • Three accounts linked before day one: one prediction market account, one crypto exchange account, one brokerage account.
    • Every trade was read from the account. Nothing was typed in.
    • Deposits and withdrawals are taken out of the return.
    • Identity check passed.
    • Statement check passed: season statements for every account this trader holds at the linked venues.
    • Account size is private. This trader agreed to share a band: $5,000 to $25,000.

    What this report can't tell you

    • Whether it lasts. Four weeks shows how someone handles risk. It does not prove an edge.
    • What happened in accounts at venues the trader never linked.
    • Why each trade was made. That is what the interview is for.
    • How this style holds up at ten times the size.
