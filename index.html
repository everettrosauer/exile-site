How it works

Three steps. Free for traders.

  1. Link your accounts

    Stocks and options, crypto, and prediction markets. Exile will read your record. It never trades for you or holds your money.

  2. Trade as usual

    Your record starts the day you link and builds every day. Nothing is backfilled. Every linked account counts, so nobody shows only the one that won.

  3. Share your record

    One page with your return, your worst drop and every trade, all in percent. Put the link on a resume or LinkedIn, or send it to a firm.

The board

Every month, linked traders are ranked on one board. It resets on the first. Fall 15% from your peak in a month and you are exiled until the next one. Pick any trader below to open their record.

Score is the month's return divided by the worst drop from peak. Risk control counts as much as profit. The bar shows how close each trader is to the line right now.

What we can't verify

  • Accounts you never link. We see what you connect. Someone could keep their losses somewhere else. That is why a trader hands over statements before a report goes to a firm.
  • Skill, from a short record. One month shows how someone handles risk and losses. It does not prove they will keep winning. The longer the record, the more it says.
  • Who is behind a wallet. A crypto wallet proves control, not identity. That is what the identity check is for.
  • Why a trade was made. We record what happened, not the thinking behind it. That is what a firm's interview is for.

    Questions

    Is anything live yet?

    Profiles are. You can create one today. Account linking is being built, so every name and number on the board is sample data. See what exists today.

    When does it start?

    Your record starts the day you link and keeps going. There is no start date and no deadline. The board resets at the start of each month.

    Can Exile trade in my account?

    No. Exile will only read your record. It will not place trades, move money or hold funds.

    Does anyone see my balance?

    No. Everything on Exile is shown in percent.

    What does exiled mean?

    Fall 15% from your peak in a month and you are off that month's board. Your record keeps going, and you are back on the board next month.

    Which markets count?

    Stocks and options, crypto, and prediction markets. One profile holds all of them. Prediction market trades on sports do not count.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. Exile is free for traders.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. Exile keeps score. It does not tell anyone what to trade, and a high rank is not a recommendation to copy anyone.

    Who can join?

    Adults 18 and over in the United States.

    Where are the full rules?

    On the rules page. They cover scoring, fair play and reports for firms.
