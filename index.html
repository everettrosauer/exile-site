How a season works

Four steps, in this order. Entry is free.

  1. Link your accounts

    Stocks and options, crypto, and prediction markets. Exile reads your record. It does not trade for you or hold your money.

  2. Declare before day one

    Every account you link counts for the whole season. Nobody gets to show only the one that won.

  3. Play four weeks

    Scores update every day. A recap lands every Sunday with the movers, the blowups and the storylines.

  4. Keep the record

    Your season becomes a verified record you can show anyone. Top Pro records are built to go in front of trading firms.

Two divisions

One is for fun. One is the real test. Both run on real trades.

Pro

For the record that counts

The line, 15% below your peak
  • Every linked account counts, for the whole season.
  • You are ranked on return against your worst drop, so risk control counts as much as profit.
  • Fall 15% from your peak and you are exiled until next season.
  • Scores are in percent. A $2,000 account can beat a $200,000 one, and nobody sees your balance.

What verified means

Verified
  • Every number comes from the account itself. Screenshots are not accepted.
  • A record starts the day you link. Nothing is backfilled.
  • Results are shown in percent. Your balance stays private.
  • Pro finalists pass an identity and statement check before any firm meets them.

Verified tells you the trades are real. The season tells you whether the trader is any good.

What we can't verify

  • Accounts you never link. We see what you connect. Someone could keep their losses somewhere else. That is why Pro finalists hand over statements before a firm meets them.
  • Skill, from four weeks. A season is short. It shows how someone handles risk and losses. It does not prove they will keep winning.
  • Who is behind a wallet. A crypto wallet proves control, not identity. Finalists pass an identity check.
  • Why a trade was made. We record what happened, not the thinking behind it. That is what a firm's interview is for.

Questions

Can Exile trade in my account?

No. Exile reads your record. It does not place trades, move money or hold funds.

Does anyone see my balance?

No. Everything on Exile is shown in percent.

What does exiled mean?

On the Pro board, falling 15% from your peak ends your season. You can come back when the next one starts.

Which markets count?

Stocks and options, crypto, and prediction markets. One profile holds all of them. Prediction market trades on sports do not count.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Entry is free for traders.

Is this investment advice?

No. Exile keeps score. It does not tell anyone what to trade, and a high rank is not a recommendation to copy anyone.

Who can play?

The pilot is for adults 18 and over in the United States.

Where are the full rules?

On the Season Zero rules page. They cover scoring, fair play and what top finishers receive.
