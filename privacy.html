Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version

  • This site collects two things: what you type into the waitlist form and, if you make one, your account and profile.
  • A profile is public. Your email and your password are not.
  • We do not sell any of it.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no ad or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete your profile yourself. Ask and we will delete everything else.

Who we are

Exile is run by Everett Rosauer. It is an early project, not yet a company.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details. Your email address, whether you are a trader or a trading firm, the markets you picked, and the time you signed up.
  • Account. If you create one: your email address and a password. The password is stored in scrambled form by our database provider. We cannot read it.
  • Profile. Only what you choose to add: your name, a handle, a short bio, an about me, a LinkedIn link, the markets you trade and a photo. Your photo is cropped and shrunk on your own device before it is sent, which also removes the location data a phone adds.
  • Server logs. Like most sites, our host records technical details of each visit, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and safe.

When you sign in, your browser keeps a sign in token in its own storage so you stay signed in. It is not an advertising cookie and it is removed when you sign out.

We do not collect account logins, balances, trades or payment details on this site. Account linking is not open yet.

How we use it

  • To show your profile to anyone who opens its link, and in the Traders section of the home page once it has been approved.
  • To write to you about Season Zero and Exile.
  • To see how many traders and firms are interested, and in which markets.

Profiles are public

Anyone on the internet can see a profile and its photo. Do not put anything in one that you want kept private. Your email address is never shown on your profile.

Every profile carries a tag that says no account is linked. Exile does not check what people write in a profile.

Who else handles it

Three companies process data for us. Vercel hosts the site. Supabase stores the waitlist, accounts, profiles and photos. ImprovMX forwards any email you send to a joinexile.com address on to our inbox at Google. We do not sell your details or share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

You can delete your profile and photo yourself, at any time, from the profile page. They come off the site right away. To remove your sign in as well, write to us.

We keep waitlist details until you ask us to delete them or until Exile shuts down. To be removed, write to hello@joinexile.com. We will delete your details within 30 days.

Before account linking opens

Linking a brokerage, crypto or prediction market account involves far more sensitive data than an email address. Before anyone can link an account, we will publish a new policy here that says exactly what is read, what is stored and who can see it.

Age

Exile is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
