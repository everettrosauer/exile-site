Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026

The short version

  • Today this site collects one thing: what you type into the waitlist form.
  • We use it to email you about Exile. We do not sell it.
  • This site sets no cookies and runs no ad or analytics trackers.
  • Ask and we will delete your details.

Who we are

Exile is run by Everett Rosauer. It is an early project, not yet a company.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details. Your email address, whether you are a trader or a trading firm, the markets you picked, and the time you signed up.
  • Server logs. Like most sites, our host records technical details of each visit, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and safe.

We do not collect account logins, balances, trades or payment details on this site. Account linking is not open yet.

How we use it

  • To write to you about Season Zero and Exile.
  • To see how many traders and firms are interested, and in which markets.

Who else handles it

Two companies process data for us: Vercel hosts the site and Supabase stores the waitlist. Both are in the United States. We do not sell your details or share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until you ask us to delete them or until Exile shuts down. To be removed, write to . We will delete your details within 30 days.

Before account linking opens

Linking a brokerage, crypto or prediction market account involves far more sensitive data than an email address. Before anyone can link an account, we will publish a new policy here that says exactly what is read, what is stored and who can see it.

Age

Exile is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
